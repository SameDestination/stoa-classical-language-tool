## How to sign

When you open your first pull request, the CLA Assistant bot will post a
comment. To sign, reply on that pull request with exactly:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

The bot records your GitHub username, the pull request, and the time of
signing. You only need to sign once; the signature covers all future
Contributions You submit to the Project.

If You are contributing on behalf of an employer or other organization, make
sure You are authorized to sign for it before signing. If the organization
prefers to sign a separate corporate agreement, contact the Maintainer before
submitting Contributions.
